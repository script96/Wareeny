\section{Completed This Cycle}

\subsection{Currency Detection}
On-device recognition of Egyptian pound banknotes (5--200 EGP, front and back) using
a custom YOLOv8m model. Handles worn and partially folded notes. Fully offline.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Value} \\
\midrule
Accuracy (clean notes)     & 94.2\% \\
Accuracy (worn / partial)  & 87.6\% \\
Inference time             & 34 ms  \\
Model size (TFLite int8)   & $\approx$12 MB \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{In-Hand Detection}
Detects when the user holds an object close to the camera and describes that specific
object instead of the full scene. Uses bounding-box proximity heuristics to isolate
the held item before passing the cropped region to the vision pipeline.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Value} \\
\midrule
Detection accuracy   & 89.3\% \\
Trigger latency      & $\approx$120 ms \\
False trigger rate   & 4.1\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Local Brand Detection}
Recognizes Egyptian and regional product brands from packaging and logos.
Custom YOLOv8s model covering 63 brand classes across food, cleaning, and pharmacy.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lc}

\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Value} \\

mAP50              & 86.8\% \\
Inference time     & 28 ms  \\

\subsection{Object Finder}

User specifies a target object by voice and the app actively scans the camera feed
until it is found.

\textbf{Detection pipeline:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fast path (on-device):} YOLO handles 80+ known classes at $\approx$30 ms/frame.
  \item \textbf{Smart path (cloud):} Objects outside the YOLO vocabulary --- or when
        on-device confidence is low --- are routed to the \textbf{Groq LLaMA Vision API}.
        This is the primary path for Object Finder since most user-specified targets
        (keys, glasses, wallet, medicine, etc.) fall outside the standard model classes.
        The API is queried every 3 seconds with the current camera frame.
  \item On detection: direction (left/center/right) and distance (close/far) announced
        via audio; haptic pulse confirms.
  \item Guidance cue every 30 s if not found; 2-minute timeout.
\end{itemize}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Value} \\
\midrule
Detection rate (object in frame)        & 91.4\% \\
Median time-to-detect (object visible)  & $\approx$1.8 s \\
Smart path (cloud) accuracy             & 88.0\% \\
False positive rate                     & 3.7\%  \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{Other Improvements}

\textbf{Expanded Object Vocabulary.}
General detection model fine-tuned to 153 classes (up from 80 COCO), adding
everyday Egyptian context: street food, household items, pharmacy products,
outdoor landmarks. mAP50: \textbf{91.2\%}.

\textbf{Cloud Vision Fallback.}
When on-device confidence falls below 0.25, the frame is sent to Groq LLaMA Vision
for a natural-language description. Triggers in $\approx$3\% of standard detection
cycles; used heavily by Object Finder for out-of-vocabulary targets.

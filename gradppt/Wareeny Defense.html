Wareeny·02 · Introduction
What is Wareeny?

The eyes of a blind user.

Wareeny is a Flutter-based mobile assistant that sees through the phone camera, understands the scene in context, and speaks only what matters — not every pixel, just the facts.

It unifies vision, language, audio and haptics into one voice-driven app with full English & Arabic (RTL + TTS) support — so users interact with their environment without sighted assistance.

Three core pillars
◆

Audio-first UX — every action has spoken feedback; the user may never look at the screen.

◆

Bilingual EN / AR — Arabic-script auto-detection, RTL layout and Egyptian-Arabic voice output.

◆

Context-aware AI — a vision-language core reports relevant facts, not robotic verbatim dumps.

Wareeny·04 · Problem Statement
The core problem

Blind and low-vision users lack a single, reliable mobile system that provides real-time, contextual understanding of their surroundings. Existing solutions handle isolated functions, fail in cluttered environments, do not prioritise safety-critical information, and offer limited privacy control.

The result — four daily failures
🧩
Fragmented tools
Users juggle several single-purpose apps for one outing.
🧠
Cognitive overload
Verbose narration buries the one fact that matters.
⚠️
No safety priority
Obstacle and hazard alerts wait behind chatty descriptions.
🔗
Lost independence
Higher daily risk and continued reliance on sighted help.
Wareeny·05 · Research Gap
Four gaps current solutions leave open

What the field has not solved — yet.

01
Geo-Linguistic Disparity
Western-trained models misread Arabic script and fail on Egyptian currency, local products and MENA environments.
02
Legacy CV Architectures
Pre-2023 cloud models add 0.8–3 s latency and act as simple taggers with no semantic, safety-aware reasoning.
03
The Privacy–Utility Paradox
Users are forced to choose between accurate cloud apps that expose private data and offline tools that barely function.
04
No Interactive Context
Systems give one-way descriptions, with no follow-up questions and no filtering of safety-critical information.

Wareeny·08 · Comparison · Techniques
Design choices versus the conventional approach

What we chose — and why

Verbatim OCR dump
→
Context-aware VLM reading
Reads relevant facts in a natural spoken style instead of every character on the page.
Cloud-only inference
→
Hybrid edge-cloud routing
Latency-critical safety runs locally; heavy reasoning offloads only when the user permits.
Static image captioning
→
Interactive VQA + token streaming
Speech starts mid-generation; the user can ask follow-up questions in real time.
One general model
→
Specialised + general tiers
A dedicated EGP banknote detector handles money; the VLM handles everything else.
